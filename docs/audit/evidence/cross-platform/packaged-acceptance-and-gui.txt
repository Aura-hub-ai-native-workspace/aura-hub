Method correction (recorded honestly)
   My first acceptance-C attempt used a helper that finds processes by the AppImage's executable path and signals that tree. With this
   artifact the aura-hub process was not a descendant of the AppImage runtime, so the helper signalled only the runtime and left aura-hub
   (and its Python child) running; that run was invalid and was discarded. The leftover tree was identified by DISPLAY=:98 and an
   AURA_HOME under my scratch directory, did not react to SIGTERM (its Node child was a zombie, its main thread blocked in a pipe write,
   other threads in do_exit), and was stopped with SIGKILL after re-verifying that identity. I could not establish the cause: it is
   plausible that tearing down the AppImage runtime (which owns the mount the app runs from) while the app was running caused it, and
   graceful SIGTERM to aura-hub on a healthy app exits cleanly (item 3). It is therefore recorded as an UNCONFIRMED observation, not a
   product defect. It was not reproduced through the supported quit path.
